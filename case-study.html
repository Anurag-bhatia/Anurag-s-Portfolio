Backstory

What was Loop Health solving?

Loop Health is India's fastest-growing group-health insurtech — selling to ~600 mid-market and enterprise accounts, servicing roughly two million covered lives. The business worked. The internal operating surface did not. Claims took 9 days of median TAT; policy admin spanned four tools stitched by spreadsheets; CSAT hovered around 3.8.

The directive from the leadership team was blunt: make this product feel like one product, not a stack of workflows glued together. That became Genome — a single operating surface for the Loop Operations and HR-partner surfaces, with claims adjudication, policy edits, premium reconciliation, and member lifecycle all in one place.

Introduction

What is Genome?

Genome is the internal + partner-facing admin surface for every Loop policy. Ops agents use it to adjudicate claims and reconcile premium. HR managers at client companies use it to add/remove members, run endorsements, and track utilisation. Member support uses it as a single source of truth for the state of any claim.

Platform Rollout

The hardest part wasn't the design — it was the staged rollout across a live book of business. We shadowed every claim for 30 days, dual-ran policy edits for 60, and only sunset the legacy tool after three consecutive weeks of green ops metrics.

62% ↓ TAT within 11 weeks of go-live — audited against the pre-launch claims cohort.
